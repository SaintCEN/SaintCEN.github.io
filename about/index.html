WHOamI?

I explore the point where visual intelligence, generative systems and practical engineering meet.

(About Me)

My work follows a simple instinct: understand how machines perceive, then turn that understanding into systems that are useful, expressive and reliable.

Research notes are where unfinished ideas become clear. Projects are where those ideas meet constraints, interfaces and real users.

When the technical world becomes too abstract, photography and writing bring attention back to people, places and lived experience.
